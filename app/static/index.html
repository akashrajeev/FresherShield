How FresherShield decides

Every job is scored on two things: how well it fits you, and how likely it is to be a scam.

  1. Find: Google Jobs (SerpApi google_jobs) for your role and city, filtered for fresher-friendly wording.
  2. Match: skills from your resume vs skills the posting asks for. You see what matched and what's missing.
  3. Posting red flags (free): fees or deposits, WhatsApp-only recruiting, gmail recruiters, "no interview" promises, typing/data-entry earn-per-day pitches, unrealistic pay.
  4. Web footprint (3 SerpApi searches, cached): Google and Bing searches for the company next to scam/fraud/complaint words, plus a Google check for a Knowledge Graph entry, employee reviews and an official website.

Big companies often show up with "fake offer letter" warnings because scammers use their names. FresherShield labels that as impersonation risk instead of calling the company a scam.

This is a triage tool, not a verdict. Always check the official careers page, and never pay to get a job. Report fraud at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
